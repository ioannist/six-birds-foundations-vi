\section{What this supplement holds}
\label{supp:about}

This supplement accompanies \emph{Six Birds Foundations VI: A Catalog of Dynamical Structural Laws}.
The main paper states and proves every result; its numbered items are cited here by their numbers
there. The supplement gives the Lean counterpart of each numbered item (\Cref{supp:concordance}),
the case analysis and instance list of each law (\Cref{supp:laws}), the register of
non-implications with their separating examples (\Cref{supp:nonimpl}), the finite computations
(\Cref{supp:evidence}), the axioms used by the Lean development (\Cref{supp:status}), the problems the laws
come from (\Cref{supp:method}) and the commands that rebuild everything (\Cref{supp:replay}).

Every declaration named in \Cref{supp:concordance,supp:status} is present at the file and line
given.

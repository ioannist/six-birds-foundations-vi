\subsection{Case analyses}

For each law, the table lists the principal cases a system can fall into and what settles a system
in each case. Only the cases for G1 are exhaustive; for the other laws a system may fall outside
every listed case, and then nothing in this paper applies to it. A case in which nothing has been proved is recorded as
undetermined, never as a failure of the law.

\subsection{Instances}

\Cref{tab:instances} lists five or six systems for each entry. The grades are those of the main paper:
\emph{checked} (proved in the main paper and in Lean), \emph{written} (proved in the main paper
only), \emph{imported} (a cited
theorem), \emph{open} (the question is unsolved), and \emph{analogy}: the system has the shape of
the law, but its hypotheses have not been checked for a precise model, and nothing is claimed about
it.

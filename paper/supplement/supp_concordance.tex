\section{Source concordance}
\label{supp:concordance}

\Cref{tab:concordance} lists every numbered result of the main paper with its law, its part of the
law, the line of the theorem catalog where that law's statement begins, and its Lean declarations
with their kind, file and line. Declaration names omit the common prefix of the library. Items argued on paper only are marked as such.
\Cref{tab:catalog-index} gives, for each law, the status recorded in the catalog and the status
under which the main paper states it; where they differ, the main paper's reading is the one
supported by the Lean statement.
